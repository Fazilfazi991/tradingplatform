## Short description

Verified Edge is an evidence-based market research platform that brings technical, historical,
event, macro, fundamental, sentiment, and positioning perspectives into one transparent view. It
shows contradictions, provenance, uncertainty, and when the responsible answer is to abstain.

## Long description

Markets rarely offer one clean signal. Verified Edge is designed to organize different forms of
market evidence without pretending uncertainty has disappeared. Its Evidence Fusion experience
compares seven research lenses, highlights agreement and contradiction, and explains what is
available, stale, experimental, or insufficient.

The validation process deliberately separates discovery, walk-forward research, a sealed holdout,
and prospective paper validation. Current forecast experiences are clearly marked synthetic demos;
customer predictions, recommendations, target prices, and broker execution are not active.

## Founder/demo talking points

1. Start with the problem: conventional dashboards show many numbers but often hide evidence
   quality, provenance, and disagreement.
2. Show one synthetic stock demo and point to the dominant demo label before discussing any signal.
3. Open Evidence Fusion and demonstrate that insufficient evidence produces `ABSTAIN`.
4. Explain “Backtested isn’t verified” and the independent forward-validation gate.
5. Close on transparency: what the system knows, what it does not know, and what would change the
   view. Do not discuss returns or imply a launch date for predictive features.

## Launch-post draft

We’re building Verified Edge, a transparent market-research platform designed around evidence,
contradiction, and uncertainty. Instead of turning every input into a call, it can show when the
evidence is incomplete and abstain. The public experience explains the methodology and includes
clearly labelled synthetic demonstrations. Predictive research remains internal and must complete
prospective validation before any customer feature is considered.

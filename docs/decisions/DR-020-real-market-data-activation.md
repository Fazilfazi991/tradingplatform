# DR-020: Real market-data activation

**Status:** Accepted for internal research on 2026-09-07.

Upstox is the first live read-only market-data provider for internal Verified Edge research. Provider
access does not imply unrestricted public redistribution rights. Technical and Historical intelligence
may use real market observations before formal predictive validation.

Current NIFTY 200 membership cannot be used as historical point-in-time membership. Backfilled and
forward-collected observations remain distinguishable, raw data remains immutable, and adjustments
remain explicit. A real market dataset establishes data plumbing—not predictive edge.

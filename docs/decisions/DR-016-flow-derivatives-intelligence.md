# DR-016 — Flow and Derivatives Intelligence

Status: Accepted

Flow and derivatives intelligence describe observable capital activity, volatility pricing, open
interest and positioning. They must not be interpreted as future return probability without independent
predictive validation.

Conventional labels such as long buildup and short covering remain heuristic interpretations, not factual
investor intent. PCR, OI, basis, IV, skew, delivery and institutional flow remain separate, expiry-aware
evidence. No source activation, combined score, ranking, alert, broker execution or return optimization is
authorized. Research mode remains `ENGINEERING_FIXTURE`.

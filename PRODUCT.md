# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, TypeScript, React, and Tailwind CSS, explicitly selected for the public product prototype.

## Users

Public market participants who want to evaluate transparent, multi-source probabilistic market
intelligence without using an execution terminal or receiving simplistic buy/sell calls.

## Product Purpose

VERIFIED EDGE makes a future market-prediction system understandable and inspectable. It gathers
different classes of market evidence, lets specialist engines interpret them, combines their views,
and exposes disagreement, uncertainty, provenance, and conditions that would change an outlook.

## Positioning

The product distinguishes prediction score, evidence agreement, model certainty, and data quality;
it shows why specialist engines agree or disagree instead of reducing intelligence to “AI says buy.”

## Operating Context

Users scan the broad market, compare time-horizon forecasts, investigate an individual security's
evidence stack, inspect historical analogues and non-price intelligence, and verify model/data health.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- This prototype uses synthetic fixture data and has no validated live prediction model.
- It is not a broker, execution system, portfolio manager, or advice product.
- No orders, broker connection, login, payments, alerts, or performance claims are permitted.
- Upstox live validation, PostgreSQL runtime verification, historical index membership, corporate
  actions, and independent-source reconciliation remain blocked or deferred.
- Demo, prototype, unavailable, and validated states must be unambiguous.

## Brand Commitments

- Working name: VERIFIED EDGE.
- Supporting description: Market Prediction Intelligence.
- Philosophy: “Backtested isn’t verified.”
- The working brand is not yet permanent.
- Product voice is precise, evidence-led, candid about uncertainty, and never sensational.
- The requested initial world is a premium modern dark intelligence interface with restrained color;
  green and red are reserved for genuine directional meaning.

## Evidence on Hand

- Batch 1.1 verified current NIFTY 200 exact mapping at 200/200.
- The repository test suite passed 38 tests with 90.11% statement coverage at Batch 1.1.
- No live market feed, validated probabilities, licensed corporate-action feed, independent second
  source, testimonials, performance evidence, or real-time news is available and none may be implied.

## Product Principles

1. Explain the evidence, contradictions, uncertainty, and provenance behind every outlook.
2. Label synthetic data so clearly that it cannot be mistaken for a live prediction.
3. Keep prediction intelligence distinct from brokerage, execution, and financial advice.
4. Make research readiness and unavailable sources visible rather than hiding incomplete systems.
5. Prefer calibrated probability and falsifiable view-change conditions over false certainty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public web product must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic structure, reduced
motion preferences, readable contrast, and responsive operation from mobile through wide desktop.

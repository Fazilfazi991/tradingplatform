# Commercial Market-Data Rights Questionnaire — Zerodha Kite Connect

**Subject:** Business/platform agreement enquiry — NSE EOD research and paid analytics SaaS

We understand the public Kite terms and support guidance prohibit displaying or redistributing Kite market data on external platforms and direct commercial services to an exchange-authorized route. We are therefore requesting the available **business/platform agreement**, not permission inferred from a ₹500 retail Connect subscription.

Our proposed V1 covers NIFTY 200 daily/EOD, non-personalized paper research, no execution. For every Yes, identify the contract clause, required NSE/NSE Data authorization, delay, attribution, end-user/location fees, exchange fees, user reporting, audit obligations, retention/deletion, geography, entitlements and redistribution limits. Confirm whether Zerodha supplies licensed data or requires an authorized vendor, and provide the applicable agreements, pricing, SLA/correction process and business contact.

Also confirm current history availability/interval constraints, OI/index coverage, REST limits, WebSocket/connection/instrument limits, token expiry/approved-platform refresh tokens, sandbox data limitations and whether the public prohibition on virtual/mock trading affects internal paper-signal testing. Written contractual confirmation is required.

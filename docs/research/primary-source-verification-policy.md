# Primary-Source Verification Policy

Verification searches in this order: exchange filing, regulator, company investor relations or
official release, government agency, statistics agency, central bank, original issuer, then reputable
secondary reporting.

Finding an official URL does not grant collection or retention rights. Record source type, official
status, access method, API/feed availability, automation suitability, retention implications,
internal/commercial/redistribution status, and review state. Unknown rights remain
`SOURCE_RIGHTS_REVIEW`.

When primary evidence contradicts a secondary claim, preserve both references and create a
contradiction candidate. Do not silently replace history. Publication, observation, and availability
times must remain causal; unknown publication time stays null and discovery is never backdated.

False primary-source matches, syndicated copies, recycled stories, and changed filings are explicit
regression scenarios. Primary-source verification remains research metadata until the normal
collector pipeline accepts the source and document under its existing policy.

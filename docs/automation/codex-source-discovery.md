# Codex source discovery

Every proposal records category, official status, access method, terms, potential information and
predictive role, overlap, cadence, cost, difficulty, legal status, and one recommendation: `REJECT`,
`INVESTIGATE`, `FIXTURE_ONLY`, or `APPROVE_FOR_INTERNAL_REVIEW`.

Discovery never activates a source. NSE/BSE, MOSPI, PIB, company IR, index updates, and additional RBI
datasets remain candidates until access, retention, polling, ownership, allowlist, and parser approval
are documented.

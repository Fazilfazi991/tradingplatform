# DR-017 — Evidence Fusion V0

Status: Accepted

Evidence Fusion V0 combines specialist intelligence into an explainable evidence state. It does not
create or imply future return probability.

Permanent engine weights may not be assigned before real predictive validation. Agreement among
specialist engines is not probability. Correlated evidence must be discounted. Abstention is a
first-class result. Learned fusion remains disabled and may later train only on out-of-sample specialist
outputs. Research mode remains `ENGINEERING_FIXTURE`.

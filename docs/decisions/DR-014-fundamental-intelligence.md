# DR-014 — Fundamental Intelligence

Status: Accepted

Fundamental intelligence describes business quality, financial condition, earnings development,
valuation context and company expectations. It must not be presented as future stock-return probability
until separately validated.

We preserve filing-time availability, immutable originals and separately timed restatements. Guidance,
consensus, valuation, price reaction, news sentiment and accounting facts are distinct evidence classes.
Industry contracts are extensible, and warnings use cautious review language. The engine is fixture-first;
no questionable scraper, fabricated consensus, return model, ranking, broker connection or public stock
page change is authorized by this decision.

# Flow and Derivatives Source Policy

Candidates are NSE FII/DII CSV reports, NSE F&O reports, NSDL FPI reports, and BSE bulk/block deal
reports. Zero sources are activated.

For every candidate, activation requires official status, approved file/API mechanism, automation
permission, retention, internal research and commercial rights, redistribution limits, rate limits,
historical depth and timestamp semantics. Public visibility does not establish these rights.

Dynamic NSE option-chain pages must not be browser-scraped. CAPTCHA bypass, control circumvention,
header spoofing and unofficial endpoint exploitation are prohibited. Only approved files, APIs, feeds,
licensed vendors or fixtures may be used.

# Flow Taxonomy

Participants include FII/FPI, DII, mutual fund, insurance, proprietary, retail, client, foreign and
domestic institutions, and unknown. Identity is never inferred beyond source evidence.

Flow types include cash, index/stock futures and options, ETF, mutual fund, block/bulk deal, delivery,
and other. Buy, sell, net, gross, quantity, currency, represented period, publication time and source
remain explicit.

Normalized metrics may include net flow divided by rolling median gross flow, percentile, z-score,
persistence, acceleration and cumulative flow when enough comparable history exists. Thresholds are not
optimized against returns. Delivery is participation, not proof of accumulation; block and bulk deal
intent remains unknown.

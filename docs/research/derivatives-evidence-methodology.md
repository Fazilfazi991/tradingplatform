# Derivatives Evidence Methodology

Basis equals futures close minus time-aligned spot. Premium, discount, widening and narrowing are
descriptive and expiry-aware. OI level, change, percentile, acceleration and volume/OI require contract
and expiry context. Price/OI quadrants are observations; conventional “long buildup”, “short covering”,
“short buildup” and “long unwinding” labels are heuristic interpretations, never source facts.

Rollover retains near/next expiry OI, activity, percentage, concentration and timing. Raw OI is not
compared across expiries without normalization. Dealer gamma remains `UNKNOWN` until complete legitimate
data and a validated methodology exist.

Positioning ambiguity includes hedging, market making, rolls, structured exposure and rebalancing.
Contradictions are preserved and reduce certainty.

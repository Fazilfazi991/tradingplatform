# Narrative Intelligence

A narrative requires supporting event IDs, scope, topic, summary, first/last seen times, strength,
velocity, persistence, confirmation, source diversity, sentiment, contradictions and provenance.

Lifecycle states are `EMERGING`, `ACCELERATING`, `ESTABLISHED`, `SATURATED`, `WEAKENING`, `REVERSING`,
`DORMANT`, and `UNKNOWN`. Persistence requires continued novel evidence, diversity, consistency and
attention retention; duplicate syndication does not count. Reversal retains the parent narrative lineage
and explicit contradicting evidence rather than silently overwriting the earlier state.

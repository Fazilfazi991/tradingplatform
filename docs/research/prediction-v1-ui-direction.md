## Thesis

Make the research boundary more memorable than the result. A user leaving the first viewport should recall a sealed internal experiment, not a forecast.

## Own world

Inherit the Evidence Lens: near-black matte working surfaces, editorial serif interpretation, compact sans operation, monospace provenance, amber for research attention, and direction colors only for genuine directional evidence.

## Story

Boundary first; sealed status second; sample and walk-forward evidence next; null controls and limitations before the closing prohibition. Positive results never displace caveats.

## First viewport

Show the internal-use prohibition and the candidate-status/holdout plate together. Registration and dataset hashes authenticate the study. No probability hero, glossy signal, action button, or recommendation appears.

## Form

An internal audit ledger: shared-rule metric rows on desktop, labelled stacked measurements on mobile, flat tonal depth, readable machine-state tokens, and one restrained amber seal. The signature interaction is progressive responsive reflow without hiding evidence.

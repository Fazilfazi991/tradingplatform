# Codex Psychology Audit

The daily internal audit reports extreme psychology, high-attention unknown events, rumour spikes,
narrative reversals, model disagreement, source-quality problems, classification drift, processing
latency and abstention. Codex may inspect evidence and propose parser, taxonomy or source-health fixes.

It may not change production prediction logic, activate sources, publish rankings, send customer alerts,
optimize thresholds against returns, or convert sentiment into recommendations.

# Accounting-quality Warnings

Warnings are explainable review indicators, never fraud accusations.

- `CASH_FLOW_DIVERGENCE`: CFO is materially below positive PAT.
- `RECEIVABLE_STRESS`: comparable receivable growth materially exceeds revenue growth.
- `INVENTORY_BUILD`: inventory growth materially exceeds sales growth.
- `LEVERAGE_INCREASE` / `INTEREST_COVERAGE_WEAK`: balance-sheet risk requires review.
- `MARGIN_COMPRESSION`, `DILUTION`, `GUIDANCE_CUT`: adverse movement with linked evidence.
- `ONE_OFF_PROFIT_DEPENDENCE` / `HIGH_OTHER_INCOME`: recurring quality is uncertain.
- `RESTATEMENT`: original and later value remain separate, with timing and reason.
- `DATA_INCOMPLETE`: evidence is insufficient to classify.

Acceptable language is “potential accounting-quality concern”, “unusual pattern”, “requires review”,
or “insufficient information”. Each warning retains source observation/document IDs.

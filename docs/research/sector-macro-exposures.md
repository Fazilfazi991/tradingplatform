# Sector macro exposures

Version 1 defines explicit qualitative sensitivities for banks, IT, pharma, auto, FMCG, metals,
energy, real estate, infrastructure, airlines, paints and logistics. Dimensions include rates, FX,
crude, commodities, domestic/global growth, liquidity, inflation and government spending.

Crude exposure is negative for fuel/input-intensive sectors such as airlines, paints and logistics,
and positive at the broad upstream-energy level. Company outcomes can differ; the map must not be
used as a sector recommendation or improvised by an LLM.

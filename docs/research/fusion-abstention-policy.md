# Fusion Abstention Policy

Abstention is mandatory for insufficient high-quality evidence, strong engine conflict, excessive missing
engines, stale evidence, low causal quality, excessive dependency, model disagreement, regime uncertainty,
insufficient sample or data-quality failure.

Missing is not neutral. Failed is not adverse. Stale strongly oriented evidence cannot dominate fresh
evidence. Abstention reasons are structured, user-visible and included in snapshot hashing.

Resolving abstention requires evidence-state changes—fresh independent evidence, restored causal quality,
or resolved contradictions—not a lower decision threshold.

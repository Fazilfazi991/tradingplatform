# Fusion Quality Framework

Quality is independent of direction and combines source quality, data quality, coverage, freshness,
specialist certainty, causal integrity, sample adequacy and validation status. Categories are very high,
high, medium, low, very low and unknown.

Stale, insufficient or failed engines are capped at very low eligibility quality. Specialist predictive
status remains `ENGINEERING_ONLY / NOT_PREDICTIVELY_VALIDATED`. Fixture success establishes software
behaviour, not market edge.

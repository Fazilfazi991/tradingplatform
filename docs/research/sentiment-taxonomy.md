# Sentiment Taxonomy

Types: news, event, management, analyst, regulatory and macro tone; social, attention, market-reaction
and other sentiment. Social and market reaction remain unavailable unless an approved source exists.

Directions are strongly/slightly positive or negative, positive, negative, neutral, mixed and unknown.
Binary classification is prohibited. Every observation records strength, certainty, relevance, source
reliability, novelty, confirmation, attention weight, scope, causal timestamps, analyzer version,
evidence references and hashes.

Rumour is a separate flag and receives low confirmation weight. Repetition never promotes a rumour to
fact. Source factual authority affects reliability but does not dictate bullish or bearish tone.

# DR-013: Macro and global market intelligence

Status: Accepted for internal engineering  
Date: 2026-08-30

Macro/global intelligence describes the economic and cross-market information state. It must not
be presented as stock-return probability until predictive value is independently validated.

Original releases, expectations and revisions retain their own causal availability. Cross-market
contradictions remain explicit. Sector exposures are versioned policy metadata, not recommendations.
Global market prices remain fixtures without an approved provider.

RBI RSS may provide official event metadata; it does not by itself justify numeric policy, inflation,
growth or liquidity state. Other candidates require source-specific approval. Research mode remains
`ENGINEERING_FIXTURE`; fusion, real predictions and customer publication remain blocked.

# DR-015 — Psychology and Sentiment Intelligence

Status: Accepted

Psychology and sentiment intelligence describe observable attention, narrative, uncertainty and
behavioural conditions. Sentiment must never automatically become future return probability or a
recommendation.

We use causal timestamps, evidence-bound derived observations, cluster-first duplicate suppression,
explicit rumour handling, multi-input fear/euphoria states, disagreement penalties and abstention.
Social sources remain disabled pending explicit rights and privacy approval. Research mode remains
`ENGINEERING_FIXTURE`; rankings, customer alerts, evidence fusion, trading and return optimization remain
out of scope.

# Event materiality

Materiality describes potential company, sector or market significance: `IMMATERIAL`, `LOW`,
`MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `MATERIAL`, `SYSTEMIC` or `UNKNOWN`. Relevant evidence may include exposure,
balance-sheet size, strategic/regulatory significance, duration and reversibility. Missing evidence
produces `UNKNOWN`; no quantity is invented.

Novelty is evaluated against canonical clusters and distinguishes first disclosure, new facts,
meaningful/minor updates, follow-ups, recaps, duplicates, corrections and retractions. Repetition
does not increase materiality or evidence weight. High-materiality, low-certainty records require
review.

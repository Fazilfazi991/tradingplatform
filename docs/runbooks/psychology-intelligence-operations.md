# Psychology Intelligence Operations

1. Verify `ENGINEERING_FIXTURE` and internal-only output labels.
2. Process only events available by cutoff; preserve raw evidence and cluster membership.
3. Review duplicate-suppression, unknown, abstention, source-diversity and model-disagreement metrics.
4. Investigate extreme states, high-attention unknowns, rumour spikes and narrative reversals.
5. Disable a faulty adapter or analyzer without changing prediction logic; rebuild from immutable inputs.

All source text is untrusted. It cannot request tools or secrets, change schemas, alter source policy or
override evidence rules. The provider-neutral LLM router may handle routine sentiment, complex tone,
narratives, uncertainty, contradictions and rumours. High-materiality ambiguity may use consensus, but
material model disagreement must be preserved and certainty reduced.

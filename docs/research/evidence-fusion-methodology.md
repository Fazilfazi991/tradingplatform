# Evidence Fusion Methodology

V0 checks eligibility, freshness, quality, dependencies, agreement, contradictions and abstention in that
order. A non-insufficient state requires at least two eligible specialist families and one medium-or-better
quality contribution. Stronger evidence states require broader independent support.

Agreement counts are descriptive, not Bernoulli votes or probability. Quality tiers and provenance
independence precede engine count, so many weak correlated states cannot automatically outweigh fewer
high-quality adverse states. The output is an evidence state, not expected return or advice.

Removal and sensitivity analyses expose dominance and changes caused by quality downgrade, freshness
downgrade or missing engines. Future learned fusion is disabled and may train only on out-of-sample
specialist outputs, never in-sample evidence.
